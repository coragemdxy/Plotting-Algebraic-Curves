# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss technical ideas and evidence rather than personal attributes.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge that contributors have different levels of
  familiarity with Julia and real algebraic geometry.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through their
GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and addressed in a
fair and proportionate manner.
